An **MVP release** (Minimum Viable Product release)

An **MVP (Minimum Viable Product)** is an early, stripped-down version of a product released to test core assumptions and gather feedback from early adopters, whereas **GA (General Availability)** is the official, public release of a finished, stable product available to the general market.

The full form of **PRD** is Product Requirements Document. It is a blueprint that guides a product's development by defining its purpose, features, functionality, and behavior.
